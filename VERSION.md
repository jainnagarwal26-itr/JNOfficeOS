## 🎯 Primary Achievements

1. **100% Google Sheets & Apps Script Elimination**:
   - Replaced 60 interconnected spreadsheets and VLOOKUP queries with 3NF normalized PostgreSQL database schemas, Row Level Security (RLS), and atomic sequence generators.

2. **Modular Architecture Execution**:
   - **Module 1**: Database Foundation & Core DDL
   - **Module 2**: Authentication, Authorization, RLS & Session Management
   - **Module 3**: Enterprise CRM Domain (Clients, Contacts, Addresses & Full-Text Search)
   - **Module 4**: Case Management & Workflow Domain (Tasks, Timelines & Time Tracking)
   - **Module 5**: Finance, Billing & Accounting Domain (Invoices, Receipts, Expenses & Indian GST Engine)
   - **Module 6**: Document Management System (DMS Vaults, Versioning & Signed URLs)
   - **Module 7**: Notifications, Alerts & Communication Hub (Realtime & Abstract Queues)
   - **Module 8**: Automation Engine & Business Rules Platform (Pub-Sub Event Bus & Approvals)
   - **Module 9**: Executive BI, Dashboards & Analytics Reporting (Materialized Views & KPI Engine)
   - **Module 10**: Release Candidate RC-1 Final QA, Integration & System Certification
